How it works

Three short steps from "where do I start?" to a plan you can edit.

1

Introduce yourself

Tell us your name, school, major and minor in one friendly sentence, then where you are in your studies today.

2

Choose your pace

Four classes a term or five. Add the electives you want and the courses you've already finished.

3

Download your roadmap

See every term laid out with prerequisites first, then save it as an Excel workbook that tracks your progress.

What to know before you start

This is a working prototype. It builds plans from a built-in sample catalog based on the upper-division Accounting schedule for 2026-2027. Live catalog and student-record connections are planned for a later phase, so nothing here replaces a meeting with your academic advisor.
